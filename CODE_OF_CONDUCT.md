# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

SMV Security is committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the SMV Security community will be treated with respect and dignity.

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces online and offline, including repositories, mailing lists, forums, GitHub discussions, and events organized or promoted by SMV Security.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful** - Treat all community members with respect, regardless of their background, experience level, or viewpoint
- **Being constructive** - Provide helpful feedback and criticism in a professional manner
- **Being inclusive** - Welcome and support people of all backgrounds and identities
- **Being collaborative** - Work together to improve the project for everyone
- **Being patient** - Remember that everyone has different skill levels and learning curves
- **Acknowledging mistakes** - If you make a mistake, acknowledge it and learn from it
- **Asking for help** - Encourage community members to ask questions and seek guidance

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- 🚫 Harassment, bullying, or discrimination of any kind
- 🚫 Offensive comments about identity, expression, or experience
- 🚫 Unwanted sexual advances or attention
- 🚫 Trolling, deliberately disruptive comments, or personal attacks
- 🚫 Publishing others' private information without consent
- 🚫 Spamming or self-promotion without relevance
- 🚫 Advocating for or encouraging any of the above behaviors

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it by contacting the community team:

📧 **Email:** community@scolemax.co.ke
📧 **Security Issues:** security@scolemax.co.ke
📧 **Licensing Questions:** licensing@scolemax.co.ke

All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality. We are committed to protecting the identity of reporters and will not tolerate retaliation.

---

## Enforcement

Community organizers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for other behaviors they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all SMV Security community spaces:

- ✅ GitHub repositories
- ✅ Issue discussions and pull requests
- ✅ Email communications
- ✅ Community forums and chat
- ✅ Official events and conferences
- ✅ Social media accounts and comments

---

## Consequences of Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior from any community member will not be tolerated. The Community Team will take appropriate action based on the severity:

1. **Warning** - For minor violations
2. **Temporary Restriction** - For repeated or moderate violations
3. **Permanent Ban** - For severe or repeated violations
